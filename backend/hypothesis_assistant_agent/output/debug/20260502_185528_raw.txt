HYPOTHESIS [1]:
Statement: USP7 inhibition directly suppresses KRAS stability by inducing ubiquitination of KRAS, thereby preventing its phosphorylation and activation.
Addresses: GAP [1]
Rationale: This hypothesis combines evidence from Paper 1 and Paper 3, which show that USP7 inhibitors suppress NSCLC cell proliferation, particularly in cells resistant to the KRAS-G12C inhibitor AMG510. Furthermore, Paper 3 reveals that the AcCoA-NLRX1 axis promotes drug resistance through mitophagy, suggesting a potential link between USP7 and KRAS stability.
