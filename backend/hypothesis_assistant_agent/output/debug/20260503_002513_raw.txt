Statement: USP7 deubiquitinates RAS, stabilizing it and rendering KRAS-G12C inhibitors less effective in NSCLC cells.
Addresses: GAP [4]
Rationale: This hypothesis is supported by Paper 1, which shows that USP7 inhibitors suppress NSCLC cell proliferation, particularly in cells resistant to AMG510. The mechanism of USP7-mediated RAS stabilization is unknown (GAP [4]), and understanding this relationship could inform more targeted therapies. Experimental validation of the USP7-RAS intermediate would provide insight into potential therapeutic targets.

Experiment: Use an in-vitro assay to measure the effect of USP7 inhibitors on RAS stability in NSCLC cells resistant to KRAS-G12C inhibition (e.g., using a cell line from Paper 1).

HYPOTHESIS [2]:
Statement: Inhibition of BRD4 synergizes with CDK4/6 inhibition, inducing senescence and ferroptotic cell death in KRAS inhibitor-resistant NSCLC cells.
Addresses: GAP [2]
Rationale: Paper 3 demonstrates that the combination of cytostatic CDK4/6 and BRD4 inhibitors induces senescent cancer cells primed for activation of ferroptotic cell death. However, no study has tested whether this combination interacts with KRAS inhibitor resistance in NSCLC (GAP [2]). This hypothesis proposes a novel mechanism by which targeting these pathways could overcome resistance.

Experiment: Use an in-vitro assay to test the effect of BRD4 inhibition on the synergy between CDK4/6 and BRD4 inhibitors in NSCLC cells resistant to KRAS-G12C inhibition (e.g., using a cell line from Paper 3).
